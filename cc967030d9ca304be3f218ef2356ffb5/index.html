Lahn Lyrics for Mac Apple Silicon (M1 or newer)

A small menu-bar app that follows Lahn, Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music in the browser (music videos too), Anghami and others. It doesn't play music; it follows whatever is playing and shows Lahn's synced lyrics for it.

Install on a Mac

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg file and drag the app into Applications.
  2. The first time only: open Applications, right-click (or Control-click) the app and choose Open, then Open again. The apps aren't signed by Apple, so a normal double-click is blocked the first time.
  3. If macOS still says it can't be opened: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to the app's name.
  4. After that it opens normally.

Lahn Lyrics lives in the menu bar (top right). The first time it reads Spotify or Apple Music, macOS asks whether it may control that app: click OK so the lyrics stay exactly in time. If Lahn’s own lyrics are on as well, you’ll see lyrics twice for Lahn songs: turn one of them off.
